# Plans

Each area of Sjel has one plan in this folder. A plan holds the open work of its area, each item
with a done-when check, and shrinks shipped work to one line that names its commit.
[`PLAN.md`](../../PLAN.md) orders the areas by priority. What Sjel is and how it is shaped is on
the [product page](../product.md). The rules for writing a plan are in
[`on-documentation.md`](../../Packs/harness/skills/sjel/references/on-documentation.md#plans).

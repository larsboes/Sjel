# Add consented interior guidance

## Outcome

Keep RoomPlan capture usable when optional paid model guidance fails.

## Acceptance

- Provider, billing and spending approval precede any request.
- Per scan, default to Local only; selected stills require a fresh action.
- Live guidance is opt-in, samples at most every 15 seconds and at most four frames per scan, then stops.
- The Mac holds the key; frames are not archived; Interior validates every proposal before an owner saves it.

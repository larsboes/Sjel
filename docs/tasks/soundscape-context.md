# Derive Soundscape from context

## Outcome

Choose presets and parameters from calendar, presence and activity.

## Acceptance

- Agree signal mapping and manual override semantics before code.
- A calendar or presence change selects a preset without a manual step.
- Manual choice persists until its session ends; all inputs use their owning capability contracts.

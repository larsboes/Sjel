# Knowledge workflow

The vault holds durable knowledge. Sjel owns operational records, source archives and unsorted
imports. Do not recreate the old Atlas, project, journal, media or inbox trees in the vault.

## Add knowledge

1. Resolve the active vault and pass `scripts/vault contract`.
2. Search `Knowledge/` and read the owning note and domain MOC.
3. Extract the durable claim from its source. Keep the source and its attachments in Sjel when
   they are operational records or unsorted imports.
4. Update an existing concept note where possible. For a new note, follow the contract's location,
   type, maturity, summary and category rules. Link its source and related concepts where useful.
5. Check the resulting links and metadata. Verify Bases and canvases in Obsidian.

## Synthesize

Search the vault, read the notes that support each claim, and answer with wikilinks to those notes.
Write the synthesis only when it contributes durable knowledge. Add it to its owning concept note
or create a note under an existing Knowledge domain according to the contract.

## Review

Report missing required properties, orphaned notes, duplicate claims, broken links, missing domain
MOCs and stale metadata. Apply only scoped, forward-only corrections. Do not bulk-migrate notes or
invent new vault structures as a lint fix.

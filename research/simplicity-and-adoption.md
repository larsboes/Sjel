# Will people without technical experience use it?

First written 2026-09-27.

## What the sources show

**Defaults are the product.** Fewer than 5% of several hundred surveyed Word users had changed
any setting at all. Programmers were the exception
([Spool, Do users change their settings?, UIE, 2011](https://archive.uie.com/brainsparks/2011/09/14/do-users-change-their-settings/),
a practitioner survey, not peer-reviewed).

**Hiding advanced options helps.** Progressive disclosure improves learnability, efficiency of use
and error rate ([Nielsen, Progressive Disclosure, Nielsen Norman Group, 2006](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/),
no numbers given).

**People find their own things by browsing, not by asking.** Users estimated that they navigated
for 56–68% of file retrievals and searched for only 4–15%, using search "mainly as a last resort"
([Bergman, Beyth-Marom, Nachmias, Gradovitch and Whittaker, ACM TOIS 26(4), 2008](https://doi.org/10.1145/1402256.1402259)).

## Open challenge

**Owning your data is not a reason to switch.** In a study of personal data stores (a survey of
2,335 people, 10 expert reviews and 17 user tests), "user interest remains limited". People
responded best to uses that "reduce administrative burdens", and people who used a prototype
reported higher intention to use it than people who only read about it
([Theys, Mechant, De Marez and Saldien, IJHCI, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1080/10447318.2025.2545467)).

## What the sources do not show

- Whether a second household can run Sjel without help. Not measured yet.

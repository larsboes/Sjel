# Data intake boundaries

Each file has one accepting owner and one intake route. The [Data plan](../plans/data.md)
links pending import, extraction and review acceptance.

## Files and archives

Media owns immutable content-addressed local attachments in the private overlay, with source
provenance and deduplication. Mounted-volume libraries retain their separate ingest contract.
Intake excludes credentials before descent/content reads and never removes source originals.
A metadata backup is not a backup of attachment bytes.

The knowledge vault keeps knowledge and required support assets. Other material stays
preserved outside it until its owning capability accepts the batch and attachments.
Official documents remain PDF-first. Other extracted records retain hash-verified archived
originals; extraction does not authorize removal. Journal data requires its own owner rather
than remaining a Projects domain.

The import router skips development bundles and agent state. Each recognized route needs an
owner that actually accepts it: a pending receipt is not completed intake. Retries preserve
source identity and account for refused/unsupported files explicitly.

## Extraction and identity

The [mail evidence contract](../capabilities/mail-evidence.md) owns staging, provenance,
reviewed aliases and native evaluation. Source structure and scoped identifiers precede prose
interpretation. Normalize owner fields while retaining original values and provenance.

Canonical owner references and reviewed physical aliases establish identity. Product aliases
and lexical similarity retrieve candidates, not physical-instance identity. Unknown/ambiguous
mentions remain reviewable. Models produce grounded proposals rather than owner writes.
AFM is the sole generative backend for extraction/mapping; unsupported layouts abstain.

Small statistical models require reviewed failure evidence and held-out quality gates.
Feedback labels from one task cannot silently become labels for another. Retrieval-producer
changes retain vector provenance and require deliberate reindexing.
No processing-policy change alone authorizes stopping a model server or migrating unrelated roles.

Mail recognition stages minimal excerpts and source references, never full-body archives.
C3 never enters prompts; c2 stays local and must fit the target owner's classification.
Delivery may suggest ownership; orders/shipments alone cannot. Returns and cancellations need
review without erasing prior evidence. Per-message checks respect stricter thread class.
Approvals, rejections and provenance survive retries.

## Media organization and posts

Content analysis proposes tags/collections bound to immutable digest evidence. Changed evidence
starts pending; identical replay preserves terminal decisions. Similarity never authorizes
deletion or blob movement. Native analysis previews the exact frames classified. Cloud image
processing requires separate approval.

Media owns Instagram drafts and manual phone handoff. Posts owns LinkedIn draft folders and
its separately reviewed publishing work; its schema may remain network-neutral without
claiming all networks. Media receives owner-selected originals, never reads the photo library,
keeps editing external and stores no person identities or consent model.

## Browser clips

The [Clipper](../../crates/comms/clipper/README.md) owns the detached upstream fork and native
messaging transport. Comms owns full versioned clip documents; feed extraction is a projection,
not a substitute for submitted content, annotations or distinct highlight identity.
Template create/overwrite/prepend/append require explicit destinations and conflict handling.
Large capture needs bounded attachments, not only a larger global request limit.

The knowledge vault is not the raw clipping inbox. Clips stay c2 and unenriched until reviewed
routing resolves owner, classification and egress. Chrome, Safari and phone transport need
independent permission and device acceptance. Upstream tests alone do not prove parity.
Platform automation beyond visible-page capture requires a terms review.

## Learning and adaptation

Personalization follows observed offered-versus-chosen behavior, not guessed preferences.
Material quality is not learner mastery. The learning Pack and vault contract own pedagogy
and learning metadata; Sjel must not introduce a second durable learning store.

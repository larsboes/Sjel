# Compare corridor reliability

## Outcome

Report route families with measured uncertainty and sample sizes.

## Acceptance

- Compare median, P90 and deadline arrival probability from collected observations.
- Every figure includes sample size; forecasts with at least 20 rides per bucket are within 10 percentage points.
- Compare Brier score against the source prognosis on the same rides.

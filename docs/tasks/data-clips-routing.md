# Route reviewed clips to their owners

## Outcome

Route reviewed clips to their owners through the existing owner, with implementation and live acceptance recorded separately.

## Acceptance

- Route versioned clips to capability owners with provenance; knowledge results may enter the vault only through its contract.
- Keep clips c2 and unenriched until reviewed routing resolves classification/egress.
- Import historical Clippings from unsorted archive with dry-run/counts and intact originals; platform-specific automation requires terms review and remains deferred.

# Posts

Posts owns LinkedIn drafts; Media retains Instagram drafts and manual phone posting.
The schema stays network-neutral without making Posts the owner of every network. The [draft contract](../architecture/posts.md) owns file layout. Publishing remains a separate owner-approved action.

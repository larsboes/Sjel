# Method Basis and Provenance

## Controlled language

ASD-STE100 is a controlled-language standard for reducing ambiguity in technical instructions. This skill borrows its structural discipline and lexical principles but does not bundle or claim compliance with the official approved-word dictionary.

- ASD-STE100 official site: https://www.asd-ste100.org/
- ASD-STE100 downloads: https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_downloads.html

## Technical writing

Google's technical-writing guidance supports active voice, clear/short sentences, strong verbs, and editing unnecessary text.

- https://developers.google.com/tech-writing/one/active-voice
- https://developers.google.com/tech-writing/one/short-sentences
- https://developers.google.com/tech-writing/one/clear-sentences
- https://developers.google.com/tech-writing/two/editing

## Debugging and critical thinking

Google SRE describes troubleshooting as iterative hypothesis formation and testing. Platt's "Strong Inference" emphasizes alternative hypotheses and discriminating experiments. NASA decision analysis emphasizes explicit criteria, alternatives, uncertainty, and sensitivity.

- https://sre.google/sre-book/effective-troubleshooting/
- John R. Platt, "Strong Inference," Science 146(3642), 1964.
- https://www.nasa.gov/reference/6-8-decision-analysis/

## Normative requirements

RFC 2119 and RFC 8174 define precise use of MUST, SHOULD, MAY, and related requirement terms.

- https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2119.html
- https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8174.html

## Actionability

The action-oriented module is distilled from the supplied ADHD-oriented output skill: next-action-first structure, bounded steps, tangent suppression, state restatement, visible progress, concise lists, matter-of-fact errors, and one concrete next action. Standalone invocation, persistence metadata, and other packaging behavior were intentionally removed because this file is a routed submodule rather than an independent skill.

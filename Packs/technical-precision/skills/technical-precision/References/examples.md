# Focused Examples

| Problem | Before | Better | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Synonym rotation | "Verify the system. Check the connections. Confirm receipt." | Reuse one verb for the same action. | Prevents readers from inferring different operations from stylistic synonyms. |
| Nominalization | "Perform an inspection of the filter." | "Inspect the filter." | Direct verb makes actor/action explicit. |
| Phrasal verb | "Take off the access panel." | "Remove the access panel." | Literal single verb has fewer parses. |
| Tense without lost meaning | "We have received the reports." | "We received the reports." | Simple tense is clearer when current relevance is not part of the claim. |
| Modality must survive | "An error may have occurred." | "Your request may have failed." | Do not rewrite uncertainty as certainty. |
| Clause stack | "Once the job has completed and assuming no errors were raised..." | "Wait for the job to finish with no errors. Then read the output." | Separates condition and action. |
| Marketing claim | "slots seamlessly into your stack" | State the actual integration behavior/constraint. | Replace unsupported quality claims with observable facts. |

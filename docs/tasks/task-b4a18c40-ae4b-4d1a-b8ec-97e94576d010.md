# Measure split suggestions across the library

## Outcome

One Documents command reports how many split parents receive cut proposals, and by which signal, without printing names, quotes or page text.

## Acceptance

- The report runs through the owner socket over every original PDF, or over the parents of open part candidates, and prints counts per signal only.
- It replaces the shell loop over `documents suggest-splits` that measured 9 of 43 part parents with a proposal.
- Recall against reviewed part relations is reported once split decisions exist to compare against.
